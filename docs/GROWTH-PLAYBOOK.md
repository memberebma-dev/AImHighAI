## Rules that keep the accounts alive
- Reddit: aged account, mostly helpful comments with no link. Link only when someone asks for a tool or a calculator, or the sub allows it. Read each sub's rules first.
- Quora: full answers, link at the bottom as "I wrote more on this here".
- YouTube: no links in comments (they get auto-hidden). Win with a genuinely useful comment; your channel/profile carries the link.
- Pinterest: links are expected. 5–10 pins/week, keyword titles, vertical 1000×1500 images.

> 2. Fix your Google Business Profile hours and add real truck/team photos. Free, and it's where most "near me" calls start.
> 3. Text a review link the same day you fix something. Relief = 5 stars.
> 4. Make the call button the first thing on your site on a phone.
