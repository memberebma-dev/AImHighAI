Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains what AImHighAI ("we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers our website, our app, and our emails.

1. What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, business name and city when you request an audit, use a calculator, sign up, or contact us.
  • Account information: your email address and sign-in records when you create an account.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Public business information: business names, addresses, phone numbers, websites, ratings and reviews published on public directories such as Google Maps, used to find businesses that may benefit from our services.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) used for security and to keep the service working.

2. How we use it

To deliver what you asked for, run and secure the service, process payments, send service and marketing emails you agreed to, improve our product, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run AImHighAI, under contracts that limit their use of your data: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), Groq and OpenAI (AI text generation), and Treg (public business-data lookups). We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

4. Opting out

  • Email: every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Use it, or email memberebma@gmail.com with "Unsubscribe" in the subject. We honor requests within 10 business days.
  • Business listings: if your business was contacted and you don't want to hear from us again, reply "stop" or email us and we'll add you to our do-not-contact list.
  • Calls and texts: we only text or call people who have given us permission. Reply STOP to any text to stop.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and (if you live in California or another state with similar laws) to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" (we don't do either). Email memberebma@gmail.com. We'll verify your request and respond within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

6. How long we keep it

As long as your account is open or as needed to provide the service, then up to 3 years for records required by tax and legal rules, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), row-level access controls in our database, least-privilege keys, and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; see our Security page to report a problem.

8. Cookies

We use only cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and the site working. We don't use advertising cookies. If that changes, we'll update this policy and ask where required.

9. Children

AImHighAI is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above. Big changes will be emailed to account holders.
