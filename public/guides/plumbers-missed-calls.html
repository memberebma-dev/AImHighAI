The plumber who answers first wins the job

Picture it: a pipe bursts at 7:40 a.m. The homeowner Googles "plumber near me", taps the first number, and gets your voicemail because you're under a sink. They don't leave a message. They tap the next number. That plumber picks up, and the job is gone before you even see the missed call.

How big is the leak?

The numbers swing a lot depending on who measured. CallRail's January 2025 small-business benchmark put the home-services missed-call rate at about 14%. Invoca's research found 27% of calls to home-services businesses weren't answered. The exact figure for your shop is the only one that matters, and most owners guess far too low.

What callers do when you don't pick up

In a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 US consumers, only 42% said they leave a voicemail and 21% said they just call another provider. And of the missed calls that do get returned, Quo's data from 16M+ calls found about 69% get no callback within 48 hours.

Put simply: every missed call is a coin flip on whether that customer ever comes back.

5 cheap fixes you can set up this week

  1. Missed-call text back. The moment a call goes unanswered, the caller gets: "Sorry we missed you, we're on a job. What do you need help with?" That one text saves more jobs than anything else on this list. (We set this up in HighLevel, which has a 14-day free trial. Affiliate link: we may earn a commission at no cost to you.)
  2. Fix your Google Business Profile. Correct hours, real photos of your trucks and team, and your service area. It's free and it's where most "near me" calls start.
  3. Ask every happy customer for a review, the same day. Send a direct link by text while they're still relieved the water stopped.
  4. Put a "Call now" button at the top of your website on phones. If you don't have a site, even a one-page site beats none.
  5. Block 15 minutes at lunch and at 5 p.m. to return every missed call, not just the ones with voicemails. Most callers don't leave one.

Quick answers

How many calls do home-service businesses miss?

It depends on the study. CallRail's January 2025 small-business benchmark put home services at about 14% missed. Invoca's home-services data found a live person answered only about half of all inbound calls. Either way, it's more than most owners think.

Do callers leave a voicemail?

Fewer than you'd hope. In a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 US consumers, only 42% said they leave one, and 21% said they call another provider instead.

What's the fastest fix?

An automatic text back the moment a call is missed, so the caller gets a reply in seconds instead of a voicemail beep.

Statistics come from the third-party studies linked above, which measured different samples in different ways. They describe averages, not your business. Updated November 2026.
