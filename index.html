AImHighAI — AI lead generation for local service businesses

AImHighAI finds local businesses on Google Maps that are missing a website, reviews or after-hours coverage, scores each lead, matches it to the offer it is most likely to buy, writes the outreach, and bills monthly plans through Stripe.

How it works

  1. Pick a niche and a city, for example plumbers in Hattiesburg, MS.
  2. AImHighAI pulls real businesses from Google Maps with phone, website, rating and review count.
  3. Each lead is scored on real gaps and matched to one offer: website build, review growth, missed-call text-back, or local SEO.
  4. It writes outreach that cites the lead's own details, and sends only after you verify the lead.
  5. When a lead says yes, it bills a Stripe invoice or starts a monthly subscription.

Who it is for

Agencies, freelancers and marketers who sell websites, reputation management, call handling or local SEO to plumbers, HVAC companies, roofers, lawyers, dentists and other local service businesses.
