AImHighAI — Autonomous AI Sales Pipeline Engine

AImHighAI is an autonomous AI sales pipeline that discovers leads, audits their online presence, scores conversion potential, generates AI-written assets, and runs multi-step personalized email outreach until the lead converts or the sequence ends. No cold calls, no manual prospecting.

What does AImHighAI do?

AImHighAI runs a full outbound sales pipeline automatically. You point it at any market — restaurants, contractors, real estate agents, SaaS companies, professional services — and it discovers real businesses, scores their digital presence from 0–99, generates AI audit reports and preview assets, and sends personalized email sequences with follow-ups until the lead converts. The system works for any niche because lead discovery is query-driven, not vertical-specific.

How does lead discovery work?

Lead discovery first tries the Google Maps Places API for real businesses in the target niche and location. If Maps is unavailable — billing not enabled, REQUEST_DENIED, or quota exceeded — it falls back to AI-based discovery using a large language model to suggest real businesses in that market. Every lead is then deduplicated against your existing pipeline and scored on real web-presence signals like missing website, low review count, low rating, and missing phone.

How is a lead scored?

Leads are scored 0–99 based on real digital-presence signals: missing website (28 points), below-average review count (7–15 points), low rating under 3.5 (10 points), and missing phone (5 points). Higher scores indicate bigger gaps — meaning more room for your services to add value. The score is fully deterministic and shown alongside a conversion-likelihood estimate and the specific issues that drove it.

Does AImHighAI send emails on its own?

Yes — once a lead passes your threshold (default 60) and you mark it as consent-verified, AImHighAI runs a multi-step outreach sequence with personalized AI-written emails, follow-ups, and conversion tracking. Sending uses SendGrid by default with a Resend fallback. You can stop any sequence at any time from the Outreach page.

How does billing work?

AImHighAI uses Stripe to create and finalize invoices for leads you convert. The default growth package price is configurable in Settings (default $4,997). Stripe webhook events keep invoice status in sync automatically, and failed payments trigger a three-step reminder sequence. Subscription tiers unlock higher lead caps and team features.

Who is AImHighAI for?

Agency owners, B2B service providers, consultants, and sales teams who want a hands-off outbound pipeline. Works for any niche because lead discovery is query-driven. Best for service providers who can show a tangible digital-presence gap (no website, low reviews, low rating) and turn it into a paid engagement.

Key features

  • Lead Discovery — Google Maps with AI fallback across any niche and location
  • Presence Scoring — Real web-presence analysis 0–99 (website, reviews, ratings), no fake scores
  • Asset Generation — AI-generated preview sites, SEO audits, and sales scripts per lead
  • Multi-Step Outreach — Automated email sequences with follow-ups until conversion
  • Stripe Invoicing — Convert scored leads into paid invoices with one click
  • Pipeline Analytics — Full funnel visibility: leads → scored → audited → invoiced
